# Topical Authority — phenolic.kaca.co.id

## Role and boundary

Despite the hostname, phenolic.kaca.co.id is a specialist property for phenolic toilet cubicles and professionally installed toilet-partition systems. It should help owners, designers, contractors, and facility teams select, specify, install, inspect, and maintain partitions. Existing product and application routes own transactional intent. Articles must evidence rather than assume material grade or composition, water/fire/acoustic/hygiene performance, hardware capacity, dimensions, accessibility, durability, standards, warranty, or price.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and next decision. Selection links to materials and evidence; layout links to hardware and wet-area details; application pages link to survey and specification; fabrication links to installation acceptance; diagnostic pages link to cleaning, repair, renewal, or warranty. Educational pages link to commercial routes only when a reader is ready for a survey or quotation.
